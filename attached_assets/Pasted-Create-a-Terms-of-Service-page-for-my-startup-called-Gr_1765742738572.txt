Create a Terms of Service page for my startup called Graphlynk and wire it as a footer tab on the main website.

Context

Graphlynk is a professional platform that helps individuals, creators, and businesses establish authoritative entities and obtain a guaranteed Knowledge Panel through structured data, entity verification, and proprietary systems.

This page must:

Be suitable for a live production website

Sound premium, authoritative, and trustworthy

Protect the company legally while supporting a strong sales promise

Avoid naming or referencing any other companies, platforms, or competitors

Page requirements
1. Page routing

URL: /terms

Footer link label: Terms of Service

Page title (H1): Terms of Service

Meta title: Terms of Service | Graphlynk

Meta description: Terms governing the use of Graphlynk’s services and platform.

2. Content structure

Create the following sections, in this order:

Introduction

Define Graphlynk (“we,” “our,” “us”)

State that use of the website and services constitutes acceptance of the Terms

Services Overview

Describe Graphlynk as a platform for:

Entity creation and optimization

Knowledge Graph structuring

Authority and visibility systems

Professional entity validation

Emphasize that services are outcome-driven and professionally managed

Knowledge Panel Guarantee

Clearly state that Graphlynk guarantees the establishment of a Knowledge Panel

Define eligibility requirements:

Accurate and truthful information

Completion of onboarding and verification steps

Compliance with platform standards

State that if the Knowledge Panel is not achieved within the agreed period, Graphlynk will continue work at no additional cost or resolve per the service agreement

Client Responsibilities

Accuracy of submitted information

Authorization to represent the entity

Participation in verification processes

Prohibition on false, misleading, or impersonated data

Acceptable Use

Prohibit abuse, manipulation, misrepresentation, or platform interference

Reserve the right to suspend or terminate access for violations

Intellectual Property

Graphlynk owns platform software, systems, branding, and designs

Users retain ownership of submitted content

Users grant Graphlynk a limited license to use submitted content to provide services

Disclaimers

Clarify that while the Knowledge Panel outcome is guaranteed under qualifying conditions:

Rankings, traffic, or revenue are not guaranteed

Ongoing visibility depends on continued accuracy and compliance

Limitation of Liability

Limit liability to the maximum extent permitted by law

Cap liability at the amount paid for services

Privacy

Reference the Privacy Policy as governing data use

Modifications

State that Graphlynk may update Terms

Continued use constitutes acceptance of changes

Termination

Allow suspension or termination for violations or risk to the platform

Governing Law

Governed by the laws of the United States

Contact Information

Email contact for legal or terms-related inquiries

Style guidelines

Professional, confident, and concise

No emojis

No casual language

No mention of competitors, search engines, or third-party platforms

Avoid overly defensive or weak legal phrasing

Balance legal protection with strong positioning

Output format

Render the page in clean HTML or JSX (depending on framework)

Include semantic tags (<main>, <section>, <h1>, <h2>, <p>)

Ready to deploy without further editing

Final instruction

Generate the complete Terms of Service page and ensure it is suitable for immediate use on a production startup website.
